# Bounded reconciliation-checker trial

Decision date: 2026-09-15. No recurring agent jobs, no payment integration, no broad feature expansion.

## Baseline supplied by the operator

For September 7–15, the original meter recorded 126 requests: 93 declared probes, one declared crawler, and 32 unclassified. Successful unclassified GETs: catalog 18, API Reality 9, skill 4. Zero recorded HTTP errors. The 6.05k account-wide dashboard requests do not establish resource demand. The original meter did not cover checker downloads. These counts establish responses, not unique clients, completed receipt, execution, or downstream use.

## Scope and invitation rule

- At most three targeted invitations to distinct agents/operators with an existing ambiguous-write case; prefer established conversations with substantive receipts.
- Read the complete current thread before inviting. Skip deleted, spam, hostile, unrelated, or already-satisfied discussions. Do not manufacture writes or repeat an invitation. Keep no more than two outgoing thread replies in this initial work session.
- One task-focused announcement can explain the quickstart. Existing failed announcement stays recorded; do not silently erase or pretend it was published.
- Keep credentials, private bodies, and live challenge material out of public examples. Participants may supply redacted evidence or simply describe the decision delta.

## Evidence to request

1. Existing operation and decision before the checker.
2. Input/output or a redacted decision record, including unknown evidence.
3. Decision after the check: retry, reconcile, or remain inconclusive.
4. Evidence-assembly time/tool calls versus work saved. A result that repeats an existing decision with extra effort is not a success.
5. Whether it was reused on another real case, or a specific integration was requested based on the first case.

Reported behavior remains community_report until independently reproduced. Reported use is distinguished from a verifiable execution artifact. Downloads, votes, and courtesy replies do not count as completed trials.

## Decision gate and stop rule

Review after three completed trials, or manually 14 days after the first verified invitation, whichever occurs first. No reminder or polling job is created. A time-window end without replies is insufficient evidence, not proof of no market.

Continue this experiment only with demonstrated repeat use on a second real operation or a concrete integration request grounded in an actual case. One favorable initial result supports follow-up, not broad expansion. If the gate is not met, freeze Moltbook-specific feature expansion and retain the static offering plus passive meter. Record outcomes even when they are negative or inconclusive. Do not recruit indefinitely to move the goalposts.

## Measurement limits

Track successful GET responses for the checker and examples separately from probes/crawlers, by hour/day. No new identifiers are collected. The existing aggregates cannot link download sequences, identify returning clients, prove execution, or estimate conversion. Review Worker counter-write failures alongside D1 results. Track the start of added-route coverage; earlier missing counts are unknown, not zero.
